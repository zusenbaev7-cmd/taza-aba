Smog and air quality in Karakol

Taza Aba is a free app about winter smog. For Karakol it shows the current air quality index (AQI) at 13 points — districts, residential areas and suburbs — a 24-hour forecast, the best time for a walk and tips to protect yourself from smoke.

Why winter smog happens

In winter the air is polluted mainly by smoke from coal-fired stoves and boilers. When the wind is weak and it is warmer aloft than at the ground (a temperature inversion), the smoke does not disperse and lingers over the city, especially in the evening and at night. The app shows this risk in its weather forecast.

How it helps the city hall

The app has a decision dashboard: tomorrow's smog forecast with measures by AQI level (based on WHO and AirNow guidance), district priorities, winter comparison and a 30-day trend, a printable report and a one-page A4 brief, plus an A5 sheet in Kyrgyz for district newspapers. The open daily archive is free to download (CC BY 4.0).
